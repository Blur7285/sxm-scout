A calm, practical first week. Not tied to a date. "Day 1" is whenever you land, so it follows the move date you set on Home.

Scout trip is behind youYour September 12–19 scouting trip has passed. This plan is now for settling in. Use the notes boxes on each day to capture what you learn.
Where you land mattersFlights from the US arrive at Princess Juliana airport (SXM), which is on the Dutch side. You clear Dutch immigration there, then drive or take a taxi across the open land border into the French side. There is no border control on the road, so ask the préfecture how your date of entry into French territory is documented for visa validation. Verify

  • Open (or start) a French bank account; set up Social Security euro deposit when you have an IBAN (see Banking).
  • Sign the long-term lease only after the lease checklist is green (see Lease).
  • Connect EDF, water and internet in your name.
  • Find a pharmacy and a doctor; plan the first prescription renewal.
  • Update the budget with real bills.

🔑 Long-Term Rentals on the French Side

Most long-term homes are found through local agencies and community groups; vacation sites show short stays, which cost more.

How it usually works

  • Furnished vs unfurnished: "meublé" (furnished) is common for newcomers. Unfurnished ("vide") is cheaper but you buy everything.
  • Rent quoted "charges comprises" (CC) means utilities/service charges may be included; ask exactly which.
  • Application file (dossier): ID, proof of income (SSA benefit letter, bank statements), prior lease references, sometimes a guarantor.
  • Deposit: in metropolitan France, a furnished lease deposit is at most 2 months of rent. Verify
  • Paying rent: by SEPA bank transfer is typical, which means you need a euro bank account or a multi-currency account with an IBAN.

🏢 Unit & Building Livability
Check these at every viewing. Humidity, cyclone protection and power matter more here than finishes.




Inside the unit
    

  • Moisture and mold: sniff closets, check ceiling corners and behind furniture.
    • 

  • AC: number of units, age, who services them, electricity use.
    • 

  • Water: pressure, hot water type (electric/solar), whether the building has a cistern with a pump (surpresseur) or network supply only.
    • 

  • Shutters (volets) or storm panels: present, working, who closes them.
    • 

  • Pests: termites and roaches are a tropical reality; ask about treatment.
    • 



Building and surroundings
    

  • Power backup: generator for the building? Does it run lifts/pumps/your unit?
    • 

  • Drainage: ravines and low roads flood quickly in heavy rain; ask about parking and ground floors.
    • 

  • Noise: road, bars, generators, airport flight path, roosters.
    • 

  • Parking and access: day and night; steep roads can be slippery.
    • 

  • Management: who answers calls (syndic/owner), response time, WhatsApp.
    • 



Irma lessonHurricane Irma (Sept 6, 2017, Category 5) damaged about 95% of buildings on the French side, per State reports. Ask whether the building was repaired to current standards and what the insurer covered. See Storm Prep.

📑 French Lease Basics
French leases are standardized and tenant-protective in metropolitan France. How much applies in Saint-Martin needs checking.


Verify firstI could not confirm whether the 1989 French housing law (loi du 6 juillet 1989) applies as-is on Saint-Martin or whether local rules govern. Treat the points below as the general French model. Verify




General French model (furnished primary residence)
    

  • Term: typically at least 1 year (9 months for students).
    • 

  • Tenant notice: 1 month for furnished leases.
    • 

  • Deposit (dépôt de garantie): at most 2 months of rent, returned after the exit inspection.
    • 

  • Inventory and inspection: an état des lieux at entry and exit, with photos.
    • 

  • Insurance: tenants normally must insure the home (assurance habitation).

  • Deposit amount and return deadline.
    •

Rent and fees on Saint-Martin are negotiated with local agencies. A guarantor (garant) is commonly requested in France; ask whether SSA income letters and savings can substitute.

🛡️ Insurance Overview
Four different needs. Do not assume one policy covers them all.




1. International health insurance
Required for the visiteur status (health cover for the whole stay) and your real safety net. Examples of insurers selling international plans to US retirees: Cigna Global, Allianz Care, GeoBlue. This is not a recommendation: compare the area of cover (does it include the French Antilles?), evacuation, outpatient, prescriptions, pre-existing conditions and waiting periods.
Verify


2. Medical evacuation
Some health plans include it; otherwise buy a separate membership. Island hospitals stabilize; serious cases can be flown to Guadeloupe, Martinique or the US. Check the destination clause and the cost cap.


3. Home (tenant) insurance
Normally required for French tenants. After Irma, ask about cyclone cover ("catastrophe naturelle") and deductibles.
Verify


4. Car insurance
Third-party liability (responsabilité civile) is required by French law for any car. Get quotes on the island once you have a car.


MedicareOriginal Medicare generally does not cover care outside the US (narrow exceptions exist, for example certain emergency hospital care when a foreign hospital is closer than a US one; it does not cover prescriptions abroad). You can keep Part B to avoid the late-enrollment penalty (10% per full 12-month period you could have had it), but you would be paying for coverage you cannot use here. Decide with eyes open. Verify

🛂 Legal & Residency (US citizen, Saint-Martin)


I'm not a lawyer or a tax professional. This is a planning summary of public sources, not legal, tax or medical advice. Rules change and your case may differ: confirm with the official office, an immigration lawyer, or a cross-border tax professional before acting. Last verified: Oct 26, 2026.


The short versionAs a US citizen you can enter visa-free for up to 90 days. To live there, you need a long-stay visa (France-Visas), most likely the "visiteur" category for retirees without a French job, then a residence card. Saint-Martin is not in the Schengen area. Verify

Last verified: Oct 26, 2026 · Sources: US State Dept: French West Indies · France-Visas: Schengen area · service-public.gouv.fr: long-stay visa


The path, step by step


    

  1. Short visits (≤ 90 days): passport valid 6 months, a blank page, onward/return ticket, proof of lodging and means. Cash over €10,000 must be declared on entry/exit. Level 1 travel advisory. Visits cannot be chained into living there.
    2. 

  2. Long-stay visa for stays over 90 days: apply online at France-Visas, then book an appointment with the TLScontact centre. Florida applicants use TLScontact Miami (2125 Biscayne Blvd, Suite 400A, Miami FL 33137). Apply no earlier than 3 months before departure; book at least about a month ahead. Verify
    3. 

  3. Visa type: service-public lists "visiteur" under the temporary long-stay visa (more than 3 months, up to 1 year): during its validity you need no card, it does not allow work or social benefits, and at its end you must return home unless you obtain a card. The "visiteur" card page, in turn, starts from a long-stay visa. So ask the consulate exactly which visa you would receive and how renewal works from Saint-Martin. Verify
    4. 

  4. If your visa is a VLS-TS: validate it online (ANEF) within 3 months of arrival (service-public) and pay the tax; aim for the first two weeks. A different visa type carries the mention "carte de séjour à solliciter dans les 2 mois", which is another procedure. The OFII tax is about €269 per a visa guide; confirm.
    5. 

  5. Residence card "visiteur" (carte de séjour temporaire): apply through ANEF between 4 and 2 months before the end of your current document. Fees: €350 first card (€300 tax + €50 stamp), €250 renewal, €180 extra for late filing.
    6. 



What the visiteur card requires
    

  • Sufficient resources: €1,477.93 net per month (€17,735.19/year) for one person. Verify
    • 

  • Resources may be your own pensions and income, and in some cases a solvent family member's support; housing conditions are also taken into account.
    • 

  • Health insurance covering the stay.
    • 

  • A written commitment not to work in France.
    • 

  • Documents: long-stay visa/titre, full-copy birth certificate, passport, proof of address under 6 months, proof of resources (including pension proof), tax stamps, e-photo, signed commitment to republican principles, OFII medical certificate if applicable.


Last verified: Oct 26, 2026 · Sources: service-public.gouv.fr: carte "visiteur"


Remote work: the big open questionThe visiteur status forbids working in France. On Jun 23, 2026 the Interior ministry answered a parliamentary question (no. 11730) saying remote work for a foreign employer, paid and taxed abroad, with no French labour-market link, is considered non-active under French law. The tax administration's reported position differs. France has no digital-nomad visa. Get advice from an immigration lawyer before accepting a US remote job. Verify

🏦 Banking & Social Security Deposit




Keep US access
  • Travel notice and international use on cards; check which US banks restrict foreign addresses.
  • Do not rely on one US SIM for 2FA: use an authenticator app and keep a US number.
  • Use a multi-currency account (e.g. Wise) to pay euro rent and get a euro IBAN.


French bank options on Saint-Martin
Banks with branches in Marigot / Hope Estate / Howell Center include BRED-Banque Populaire, Caisse d'Epargne CEPAC, Crédit Mutuel, LCL and La Banque Postale (25 rue de la Liberté). Banque des Antilles Françaises is also listed. Verify


Social Security (SSA) direct deposit abroad
    

  • France is on SSA's International Direct Deposit list: use form SSA-1199-FR with a euro account (IBAN/BIC). Your bank completes section 3.
    • 

  • Mail it to the Federal Benefits Unit, US Embassy Paris, 4 Avenue Gabriel, 75382 Paris Cedex 08.
    • 

  • Saint-Martin is not named on SSA's list (Martinique and Sint Maarten are listed separately). Verify


Last verified: Oct 26, 2026 · Sources: SSA: International Direct Deposit · SSA-1199-FR form


FATCA and French banks
Because of FATCA, French banks must identify US persons (you sign a US self-certification, often IRS Form W-9) and report certain accounts to French tax authorities, who share with the IRS. This makes some banks cautious, but it does not by itself bar you. If a bank refuses, ask for the refusal in writing; then you can use the Banque de France "droit au compte" procedure. Verify


US reporting once you have a foreign accountFBAR (FinCEN 114) is due when your foreign accounts together exceed $10,000 at any time in the year (due Apr 15, auto-extended to Oct 15). Form 8938 for a married couple filing jointly who qualify as living abroad (tax home abroad plus bona fide residence or 330 days abroad): $400,000 at year end or $600,000 any time; lower thresholds apply to those who do not qualify. See Taxes. Verify

🧾 Taxes


I'm not a lawyer or a tax professional. This is a planning summary of public sources, not legal, tax or medical advice. Rules change and your case may differ: confirm with the official office, an immigration lawyer, or a cross-border tax professional before acting. Last verified: Oct 26, 2026.


US taxes continue
    

  • US citizens file US returns wherever they live. Qualifying US citizens living abroad get an automatic extension to June 15 to file (interest still runs from Apr 15).
    • 

  • The foreign earned income exclusion (FEIE) covers earned income only; it does not apply to Social Security benefits.
    • 

  • FBAR and Form 8938 apply to foreign accounts (see Banking).


Last verified: Oct 26, 2026 · Sources: IRS: US citizens abroad · IRS: FEIE


French / Saint-Martin tax
    

  • Saint-Martin has its own tax code and became a collectivité in 2007. Its tax office: Centre des finances publiques de Saint-Martin, 16 rue Jean-Jacques Fayel, Concordia; tel 0590 29 25 71.
    • 

  • Someone arriving from metropolitan France or a DOM becomes a Saint-Martin tax resident only after 5 years.
    • 

  • The US–France tax treaty defines "France" as the European and overseas departments. Whether it covers Saint-Martin, which has its own tax regime, is unclear.

Treaty applicability to Saint-Martin and how your Social Security and any salary would be taxed. High priority for a cross-border tax professional.

Last verified: Oct 26, 2026 · Sources: Collectivité de Saint-Martin: impôts · IRS: US–France treaty

📦 Shipping, Customs & Pets




Shipping
  • Tropical Shipping lists Saint-Martin (Galisbay port) among its routes; its Florida drop-off is in Palm Beach County (Riviera Beach).
  • Vehicles are not allowed in LCL (shared) container service.
  • For a furnished rental, a suitcase-only move is usually cheaper than freight.
Verify


Customs
  • Saint-Martin is a free port: reportedly no customs duty, VAT or octroi de mer on most imports.
  • For a real move, used personal effects can enter duty-free with a dated, signed inventory and proof of transfer of residence.
  • Medication, alcohol, vehicles, firearms and food have specific rules.
Verify


Pets
Per DAAF Guadeloupe (updated June 13, 2025), a pet from the US needs: a microchip, age over 12 weeks, a valid rabies vaccination, and an original official health certificate with proof of vaccination. The US is on the list exempt from the rabies serum titer test. The certificate must be issued or signed by the US veterinary authorities (in practice a USDA-accredited vet plus APHIS endorsement). Contact DAAF Guadeloupe first, and check your airline's rules. Careful: the USDA APHIS page titled "St. Maarten / St. Martin" describes the Dutch side rule (15 weeks, import permit from the Sint Maarten government), which is different from the French-side rule above. Verify

  • Exchange is mandatory for non-EU licences, but only possible for states on the reciprocity list. As of May 2026 the list includes Arkansas, South Carolina, Colorado, Connecticut, Delaware, Florida, Illinois, Iowa, Maryland, Massachusetts, Michigan, New Hampshire, Ohio, Oklahoma, Pennsylvania, Texas, Virginia and Wisconsin.
  • If your state is not on the list you must take the French driving exam.

🌀 Hurricanes & Storm Prep


Irma, Sept 6, 2017A Category 5 hurricane hit Saint-Martin. State reports cite 11 deaths on the French side and about 95% of buildings damaged. It reshaped building codes and insurance. Hurricane season runs June 1–Nov 30.

Last verified: Oct 26, 2026 · Sources: Guadeloupe prefecture




Météo-France vigilance
Four colors: green no vigilance, yellow be attentive, orange be very vigilant, red absolute vigilance. For cyclones, violet means stay confined at home and grey means stay careful after the storm. Four hazards: wind, rain/storms, waves-submersion, cyclone. For Saint-Martin and Saint-Barthélemy, NHC-based alert levels are also used.
